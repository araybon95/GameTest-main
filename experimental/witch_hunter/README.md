# Witch Hunter

[Painted Witch Hunter concept](concept.png)

The approved visual direction is named **Witch Hunter**, a hooded two-handed hammer fighter intended to replace the Penitent Crusader. This folder contains the experimental character design; gameplay integration and animation assets are a later step.

The user supplied a front-view image of [Bestiarum's Witch Hunter Bastion](https://bestiarumgames.com/products/witch-hunter-bastion-stl-penitent-hero) as the reference. The design carries over the hood, sealed devotional faceplate, broad armor, armored boots and long-shaft two-handed hammer. The cape is replaced with open hanging iron chains carrying carved robed figures and devotional plaques attached by rings. The hanging objects are relics rather than living figures. The hood remains cloth; the arms and legs stay readable through gaps in the chains.

The concept uses the game's painted, ink-outlined dark fantasy style, a heavy melee build, a right-facing three-quarter stance and feet planted on a level baseline. The two-handed grip must remain readable and clear of hanging chains. No miniature base or raised stepping rock is used.

This is a first design image, not an animation sheet. Front/back equipment layout, clean combat poses and any ability changes follow after visual approval.

The concept was generated from the supplied reference image. Visual review checked the two-handed grip, complete hammer and boots, retained hood/mask, absent cape and chain-mounted relics. The PNG is 1024×1536 RGBA with transparency. No production tests were needed for this isolated concept image.

The hammer face bears an engraved banishment seal inspired by the user-supplied crossed-bones sigil: a central bone X, smaller warding glyphs and a ring of runes. The engraving follows the metal face perspective. The chain-hung devotional relics, sealed mask, hood and two-handed grip retain the approved design.
