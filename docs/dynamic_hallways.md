# Side-on hallway exploration

## Exploration presentation

The compact route maps share `scripts/route_map_art.gd`: a dark survey grid, compass detail, tiled stone corridors, framed encounter symbols, blue merchant-orb badges and a gold diamond party marker. The chosen walking route uses a brighter gold trail. Combat, boss, event, camp, treasure and stair symbols have distinct silhouettes; cleared rooms use subdued colors. Corridor events use flatter frames. The same coordinate layout supplies room click targets and visible artwork.

Rooms and passages share a closer, uniform 1.25× camera with a 750-pixel scene above a compact travel desk. This enlarges actors without distorting their proportions. The unused travel skill grid is removed; hero inspection, health/stress summaries, inventory, route selection, Scout, Retreat and destination controls remain available. Dark edge silhouettes, a fading ground vignette and theme-colored divider connect the scene to the interface. Existing optional installation layers and original panorama fallback both use the same camera.

After the first fifth of a passage, a short thematic clue describes the destination: bandit boot prints, steel in the keep, prayers and drag marks for the Remade, or wing dust for moth encounters. Known cleared passages, roaming enemies, coins, camps, curios and stairs have their own hints. These are cosmetic descriptions of existing encounter data; they do not roll rewards or apply ambush modifiers. Arrival still waits for explicit interaction. Compact room maps now fit the whole floor within their bounds, with symbols scaled to each room and click targets derived from the same layout.

Hallway travel now uses a longer side-on walk with independently moving background, middle, wall, floor and foreground layers. When the optional local installation is available, the corridor layers are loaded from it at runtime; otherwise four original panoramic backgrounds cover the occupied village, chained keep, Remade sanctuary and infested apothecary. The heroes share one ground plane and retain their walking sheets, formation, names and Workshop colors.

The resting room is now a side-on party scene instead of a full-screen map. Its lower HUD has the selected hero and readable skill descriptions on the left, with connected route buttons and room actions beside a compact dungeon map on the right. Selecting another party member updates the hero display; right-click or C inspects their record. Campfires, cleared merchant orbs and available descents can be clicked in the scene or accessed with W / Enter. The standing scene does not advance passage time or resolve a destination.

Room scenes, walking passages and combat share a ground line at y=430 in the 1920×1080 canvas. Doorway textures use their exact aspect ratio with their visible bottom on that line. Both the optional installed backdrop layers and the built-in panorama fallback carry into combat, preserving the setting from the approach. Combat's right-hand HUD offers Map / Log / Items tabs; Tab switches Map and Log. Items opens read-only inspection, while consumable actions remain in the combat strip. Curio silhouettes pulse and brighten further on hover.

Choosing a connected map destination opens a passage with the party standing at its starting doorway. Hold **D / Right Arrow** to walk forward and **A / Left Arrow** to retrace the passage; release to stop. Holding the left or right half of the scene also walks in that direction. Walking back through the starting doorway returns to the map. **Space** and **Walk On / Pause Walking** offer optional automatic forward walking. Arriving stops the party: click the outlined creature, curio or doorway, click **Interact**, or press **W / Enter / E** to continue. You can back away before interacting. There is no player-facing skip button. **I** opens contextual controls; **C**, right-click or a party card opens a hero sheet; **Tab** opens inventory. Help, hero sheets and Settings block movement and footsteps. Held input is cleared when a panel opens or the window loses focus.

The footer shows each survivor's name, class, health and stress, plus the revealed floor map and the accepted route. Health, stress and gold refresh after inventory changes made while standing in a passage. Long cleared routes take longer to traverse. Destinations enter from beyond the screen near the end of travel, rather than standing beside the party throughout the walk.

Choosing a route and walking along it preserve the current room, health, gold, inventory, loot RNG, hunger and completion state. The navigation authority separates direction agreement from physical arrival. Only interacting at the reached destination commits the route, reveals neighbors and resolves its once-only exploration costs and encounter. Backtracking before entry cancels without those effects. Routes can cross cleared rooms but stop at a new encounter; revisiting cleared spaces never repeats hunger or rewards. The controller validates the run, floor, origin and every route step again at entry, preventing stale interactions after a changed run or route. Existing surprise battles, one-use corridor coins, camps, events, floor-boss entrances and final-boss completion remain in place. New directions are chosen on the map at a doorway.

The visual direction draws on the [Immersive Area Backgrounds Workshop reference](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3404150724). The four panoramas, coin pouch and small campfire are original built-in imagegen outputs. Their individual replaceable paths, exact production prompts and viewed project references are recorded in [hallway_art_provenance.json](hallway_art_provenance.json). The two small props use measured AtlasTexture regions to ignore faint alpha flecks and meet the party's ground plane; their PNGs remain intact. When replacing those props with different dimensions, update the regions in the hallway script.

Focused verification is in `tests/continuous_dungeon_smoke.gd`, `tests/hallway_navigation_smoke.gd` and `tests/dynamic_hallway_smoke.gd`, with graphical captures in `tests/dynamic_hallway_preview.gd`. The continuous-view fixture enters combat through a real route button and checks selected-hero inventory, grounded props and battle HUD tabs. The navigation fixture exercises actual engine keyboard propagation and deferred room resolution; presentation fixtures explicitly opt into automatic walking. Existing exploration, corridor, settings/save and complete-run checks cover integration with the dungeon controller. Automatic and manual save buttons are disabled during an active passage; the last cleared room remains the checkpoint.

## Optional local artwork

`scripts/local_dungeon_art.gd` reads the editable PNGs in `assets/presentation/dungeons` first. All six layered profiles are included in the repository and source ZIP. Settings (**F10**) has **Use layered dungeon artwork and painted UI**, applied when a scene is rebuilt. Preferences are stored separately in `user://local_art.cfg`; `[art] root` can point to an optional installation's `dungeons` folder for missing images and `enabled` controls loading. A missing or incomplete profile falls back to the original drawn presentation. See [the image replacement guide](../assets/presentation/README.md).

| Ashen scenery | Local corridor folder |
| --- | --- |
| Bandit road | `weald` |
| Calden keep | `crypts` |
| Infested apothecary | `warrens` |
| Path of the Beast, floors 1–3 | `darkestdungeon/quest_1` through `quest_3` |

`scripts/local_hallway_layers.gd` tiles the layers at their native proportions, uses different scrolling speeds and derives a floor strip from each wall tile. Scrolling reverses with the party. Texture caching preserves transparency and does not use gameplay RNG. The same optional loader reads the adjacent `overlays` folder's burn, bleed and poison tray icons, with the original SVG symbols as fallback. The supplied `colours` files are lookup tables, and the `fx` files use legacy Spine skeletons; those formats are not played by this PNG renderer. The character sheet uses the custom layout described in [Controls and character sheets](controls_and_character_sheets.md).

`tests/local_dungeon_art_smoke.gd` checks missing/disabled fallback, optional installed profiles, texture caching, floor alignment, layer coverage and reversal. Checks that require installed PNGs are skipped when the installation is unavailable.

Combat now recreates the painted FX motion in `scenes/combat/impact_animation.gd`: a fast directional slash, impact hold, expanding blood spray and falling fade, plus rising fire tongues. The local blood atlas attachment is animated directly when available; the original drawn wounds remain the fallback. This is a Godot recreation of the motion, rather than playback of the supplied Spine skeleton. Misses, full blocks and beneficial actions remain excluded, and the cosmetic animation does not change damage or loot RNG. Focused checks are `impact_animation_smoke.gd`, `grimdark_vfx_smoke.gd` and `combat_cinematics_smoke.gd`.

Checks cover twenty themed destinations, paused animation/footsteps, Settings/help, surviving heroes, fallback artwork, Workshop palettes, unchanged partial-travel gameplay/RNG state, narrow-map centering and the longest camera path. Navigation checks also cover reverse walking, doorway cancellation, arriving and then backing away, once-only corridor coins and ration recovery, co-op agreement, surprised enemies and invalidated routes. The complete-run milestone uses controlled encounter victories to verify routing and once-only rewards; it is not a difficulty measurement.
