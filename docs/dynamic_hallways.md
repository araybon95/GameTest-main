# Side-on hallway exploration

Hallway travel now uses a longer side-on walk with four original panoramic backgrounds: the occupied village, chained keep, Remade sanctuary and infested apothecary. Painted architecture, slow fog and faster foreground silhouettes move at separate speeds. Lanterns and dust remain alive while the party stops. The heroes share one ground plane and retain their walking sheets, formation, names and Workshop colors.

Choosing a connected map destination begins walking. Hold **D** or **Right Arrow**, or press and hold the scene, for manual movement. Release to stop; **Space** and **Walk On / Pause Walking** toggle automatic walking. **Skip Walk** reaches the destination immediately. On arrival the party stops and waits: click the outlined creature, curio or doorway, click **Interact**, or press **Enter / E** to continue. Settings temporarily stops movement and footsteps, then restores the preceding automatic/manual state.

The footer shows each survivor's name, class, health and stress, plus the revealed floor map and the accepted route. Long cleared routes take longer to traverse. Destinations enter from beyond the screen near the end of travel, rather than standing beside the party throughout the walk.

Movement remains presentation of an accepted map route. It does not change health, gold, inventory, RNG, hunger or room completion. The dungeon controller resolves the destination after the single completion signal, retaining existing surprise battles, one-use corridor coins, camps, room events and boss rules. Manual controls advance the selected passage; choosing a different route still happens on the map.

The visual direction draws on the [Immersive Area Backgrounds Workshop reference](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3404150724). The four panoramas, coin pouch and small campfire are original built-in imagegen outputs. Their individual replaceable paths, exact production prompts and viewed project references are recorded in [hallway_art_provenance.json](hallway_art_provenance.json). The two small props use measured AtlasTexture regions to ignore faint alpha flecks and meet the party's ground plane; their PNGs remain intact. When replacing those props with different dimensions, update the regions in the hallway script.

Focused verification is in `tests/dynamic_hallway_smoke.gd`, with graphical captures in `tests/dynamic_hallway_preview.gd`. The existing walking, exploration, corridor, settings/save and complete-run checks cover integration with the dungeon controller.

Seven focused smoke suites passed: dynamic hallway, ink travel, exploration polish, corridor, settings/saves, ink expansion and the complete-run milestone. Native input was also checked in the graphical renderer, including clicking Pause and then holding Right Arrow. Checks cover twenty themed destination cases, paused animation/footsteps, Settings, survivor exclusion, fallback artwork, Workshop palettes, unchanged gameplay/RNG state, narrow-map centering and the longest camera path. Ten graphical previews cover walking and arrival across all four themes. The complete-run milestone uses controlled encounter victories to verify routing and once-only rewards; it is not a difficulty measurement.
